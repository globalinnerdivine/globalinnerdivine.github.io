Japanese Microseasons: Kitakaze Konoha o Harau (朔風払葉), North Wind Blows the Leaves from the Trees

Japan's 72 microseasons, or shichijūni-kō (七十二候), divide the year into short seasons of about five days each. Now comes kitakaze konoha o harau (朔風払葉): north wind blows the leaves from the trees, around November 27 – December 1. The north wind clears the branches.
